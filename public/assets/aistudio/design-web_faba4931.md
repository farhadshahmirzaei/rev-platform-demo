The idea

## What is an AI employee platform?

An AI employee platform is business software where AI agents work as members of your team. Each AI employee has a role, permissions, a schedule and a trust level, and works in the same records as your people.

AI Ekip's AI Employee Platform is that workspace. It works like an operating system for your company: the modules are the apps, people and agents are the users, and the platform decides what each one may do.

Starter AI employeesChief of Staff, Project Manager, Finance Controller and Lead Researcher, hired from a catalogue.

Where agents runIn your own Claude app through a connector, or hosted on the platform with your own Anthropic API key.

PricingOne flat price per organization. Unlimited AI agents and team members, no seats.

Your dataIsolated per organization. The Owner can export everything as JSON or CSV, or delete it.

DeploymentCloud at platform.aiekip.com, or an Enterprise edition on your own server.

How the operating system works

## Four layers. People stay on top.

Work flows down from your team to AI employees and into your business data. Anything an agent may not decide alone flows back up as a request.

1. 01Your team decidesEverything an agent cannot decide alone arrives as a request. You approve, decline or answer, and review how each agent is doing.
2. 02Agents do the workAI employees run on schedules, work in the same records as your people and report what they did after every run.
3. 03The platform enforces the rulesLimits are not lines in a prompt. The platform checks role, trust level and caps on every call an agent makes.
4. 04One source of truthPeople and agents read and write the same customers, deals, tasks and invoices, so nobody works from a stale copy.

Trust and control

## Agents earn trust the way people do

A trust level decides what an agent may do on its own. It is not a line in a prompt: the platform checks it on every request. You raise the level when the work earns it.

Level 1 of 4

### Observe

Reads and reports. Changes nothing.

At every level, deletes and money movement need your approval.

ObserveProposeAct with approvalAutonomous

- Pause one agentStop a single agent without touching the rest.
- Kill switchStop every agent in the organization at once.
- Daily capsA limit on what each agent may do in a day.
- Spend limitHosted agents stop at your monthly limit.
